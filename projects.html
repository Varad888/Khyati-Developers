Minal Apartment 🏢

Redevelopment Project by Khyati Developers LLP

📍 Shreya Nagar Chh. Sambhajinagar

Khyati Developers LLP proudly presents Minal Apartment, a thoughtfully planned redevelopment project designed to offer modern, comfortable, and secure living spaces for today’s families. With a focus on quality construction, practical planning, and premium amenities, the project aims to redefine everyday living with convenience and elegance.

Located in a well-connected and peaceful area, Khyati's Minal Apartment combines smart space utilization with modern infrastructure to create a perfect residential environment.

✨ Project Highlights

  • Spacious & well-planned 2 BHK Luxurious Flats
  • Modern Lift with Battery Backup
  • 24×7 Watchman & Security Support
  • Dedicated Covered Parking for 4-Wheelers & 2-Wheelers
  • Solar System for Common Area Lighting & Lift Backup
  • CCTV Surveillance for Common Areas
  • Quality construction with modern design approach according to Vastu Shastra
  • Comfortable living with enhanced safety features

🌟 Why Choose Minal Apartment?

At Khyati Developers LLP, we believe in delivering homes that combine quality, comfort, and trust. Khyati Minal Apartment is planned to provide residents with a secure, energy-efficient, and future-ready lifestyle while maintaining the warmth of a family-oriented community.
